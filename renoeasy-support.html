RenoEasy Support

RenoEasy estimates contracted power capacity, connection costs and air-conditioning sizes for home renovation in Macau, from public data published by CEM. It works fully offline and collects no data.

Contact

Email: appchallengek@gmail.com — we usually reply within a few working days.

Common questions

Are the figures official? No. They are estimates based on CEM's public tariff tables and guidelines. Actual charges and approved capacity are decided by CEM.

What does Pro include? Unlimited projects and PDF reports. The free version includes one project with the full power-capacity calculation.

How do I cancel my subscription? In iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → RenoEasy.

常見問題

數字是官方的嗎？不是。數字是根據澳電公開的收費表及指引計算的估算。實際收費及獲批容量由澳電決定。

Pro 包括甚麼？不限項目數量及 PDF 報告。免費版包括一個項目及完整的電錶容量計算。

如何取消訂閱？在 iOS 設定 → 你的名字 → 訂閱 → RenoEasy 內取消。
