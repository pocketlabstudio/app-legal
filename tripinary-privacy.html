Tripinary Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Tripinary is a travel-planning app with accounts and sync. When you create an account (Sign in with Apple or Google), we receive your account identifier and, depending on the provider and your choices, your name and email address. Your trip content — itineraries, places, notes, budgets, expenses, checklists and cover photos — is stored on your device and on our sync server so it can stay in step across your devices and with the people you share a trip with.

2. AI Features

AI features (itinerary generation, AI assistant, AI editing, booking import and similar) are optional and are used only with your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings. When you use them, the relevant trip content and your request are sent to our server and processed by a third-party AI provider (Anthropic). Text that the AI refuses to process may be kept on our server for up to 14 days for abuse prevention. Please do not enter passport numbers, payment card numbers or other highly sensitive information into AI requests.

3. Sharing and Co-editing

If you invite others to a trip, or enable a public read-only link, the people with access can view (and, for invited members, edit) that trip's content. You can remove members or turn off the public link at any time in the app.

4. Photos

Cover photos and images you attach for booking import are processed to provide the feature you asked for. Screenshots submitted for booking import are sent to the AI provider to extract booking details.

5. Location

Place search and map features run on your device (Apple MapKit). When you open directions, you are handed over to the navigation app you choose (Apple Maps, Google Maps or Baidu Maps), whose own privacy policy applies.

6. Advertising (Free Version)

The free version offers optional rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob — you choose whether to watch an ad to earn an AI conversation. To serve ads, Google may collect device information, an advertising identifier (only with your consent under Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt), IP address and ad interaction data, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We verify completed views on our server to credit your account. Subscribers see no ads.

7. Subscriptions and Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive your subscription status from Apple so the app can unlock subscriber features; we never receive or store your payment details.

8. Data Retention and Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings › Delete Account). Deleting your account removes your account and your trip data from our server, except data we must keep briefly for security or legal reasons. Deleting the app removes its local data from your device. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription — subscriptions are managed in your Apple ID settings.

9. Children

Tripinary is intended for adults planning travel and is not directed at children.

10. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version will always be posted on this page. Questions: appchallengek@gmail.com

Tripinary 私隱政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 27 日

1. 我們收集的資料

Tripinary 是一款設有帳戶與同步功能的旅遊規劃 App。當你建立帳戶（使用 Apple 或 Google 登入），我們會收到你的帳戶識別碼，以及（視乎供應商與你的選擇）你的姓名與電郵地址。你的行程內容——包括行程、景點、筆記、預算、開支、清單及封面相片——會儲存在你的裝置及我們的同步伺服器，以便在你的裝置之間及與你分享行程的人之間保持同步。

2. AI 功能

AI 功能（行程生成、AI 助手、AI 修改、預訂匯入等）屬自選功能，只會在你同意後使用，你可隨時在「設定」撤回同意。使用時，相關行程內容及你的要求會傳送至我們的伺服器，並由第三方 AI 服務供應商（Anthropic）處理。被 AI 拒絕處理的文字可能會在伺服器保留最多 14 日作防濫用之用。請勿在 AI 要求中輸入護照號碼、信用卡號碼或其他高度敏感資料。

3. 分享與共同編輯

如你邀請他人加入行程，或開啟公開唯讀連結，獲存取權的人可查看（受邀成員更可編輯）該行程的內容。你可隨時在 App 內移除成員或關閉公開連結。

4. 相片

封面相片及你為預訂匯入而附加的圖片，會為提供你所要求的功能而處理。為匯入預訂而提交的截圖會傳送至 AI 供應商以抽取預訂資料。

5. 位置

地點搜尋與地圖功能在你的裝置上運行（Apple MapKit）。當你開啟導航時，會轉至你所選的導航 App（Apple 地圖、Google 地圖或百度地圖），並受其私隱政策約束。

6. 廣告（免費版）

免費版提供由 Google AdMob 提供的自選獎勵廣告——是否觀看廣告以換取 AI 對話，完全由你決定。為提供廣告，Google 可能會收集裝置資料、廣告識別碼（只在你於 Apple「App 追蹤透明度」提示中同意後）、IP 位址及廣告互動資料，並受 Google 私隱政策約束。我們會在伺服器驗證已完成的觀看，以將額度記入你的帳戶。訂閱用戶不會看到廣告。

7. 訂閱與購買

訂閱由 Apple 經 App Store 處理。我們會從 Apple 接收你的訂閱狀態，以便 App 解鎖訂閱功能；我們不會接收或儲存你的付款資料。

8. 資料保留與刪除帳戶

你可隨時在 App 內刪除帳戶（設定 › 刪除帳戶）。刪除帳戶會將你的帳戶及行程資料從我們的伺服器移除，惟基於保安或法律原因須短暫保留的資料除外。刪除 App 會移除裝置上的本地資料。刪除帳戶並不會取消訂閱——訂閱須在你的 Apple ID 設定中管理。

9. 兒童

Tripinary 供成人規劃旅遊使用，並非以兒童為對象。

10. 修訂與聯絡

我們可能不時更新本政策，最新版本會一直張貼於本頁。查詢：appchallengek@gmail.com
