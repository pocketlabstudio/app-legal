BillSlip Support

BillSlip （開單易） by PocketLab — quotations, invoices and receipts in one app, with bilingual (Chinese/English) A4 PDFs.

Contact

Email: appchallengek@gmail.com — we usually reply within 2 business days.

Free Version and BillSlip Pro

The free version lets you create up to 5 documents in total (quotations, invoices and receipts combined, including documents created by conversion). BillSlip Pro (monthly or annual subscription, with a 7-day free trial on the annual plan) removes the limit. Prices are shown in the app for your region.

Managing Your Subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. To manage, cancel, or turn off auto-renewal: open the App Store app → tap your profile → Subscriptions → BillSlip. If your subscription expires, your existing documents stay viewable and editable and you can still record payments — you just cannot create or convert new documents until you subscribe again or are back within the free limit.

Restore Purchases

In the app, open Settings (template settings) and tap "Restore Purchases", or use the restore button on the paywall. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID used for the subscription.

Your Data

All documents are stored only on your device — there is no account and no cloud sync. Deleting the app deletes its data, so please export PDFs of anything you need to keep. See the Privacy Policy.

免費版與開單易 Pro

免費版可建立合共 5 份文件（報價單、請款單、收據合計，包括以轉換方式產生的文件）。開單易 Pro（月費或年費訂閱；年費計劃附 7 日免費試用）可解除上限。你所在地區的價格會在 app 內顯示。

管理訂閱

訂閱由 Apple 處理。如要管理、取消或關閉自動續訂：開啟 App Store app → 點按你的頭像 → 訂閱 → BillSlip。訂閱到期後，你現有的文件仍然可以檢視和編輯，也可以繼續記錄收款；只是不能建立或轉換新文件，直至你再次訂閱或回到免費上限之內。

恢復購買

在 app 內開啟設定（模版設定）點按「恢復購買」，或使用付費牆上的恢復按鈕。請確保你登入的是訂閱時使用的 Apple ID。

你的資料

所有文件只儲存在你的裝置上 — 沒有帳戶、沒有雲端同步。刪除 app 會一併刪除資料，請先匯出需要保留的文件 PDF。詳見私隱政策。
