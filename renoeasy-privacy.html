RenoEasy Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

1. What the app does

RenoEasy helps you estimate the contracted power supply capacity, connection costs and air-conditioning sizes for home renovation in Macau, based on public tariff and guideline data published by CEM (Companhia de Electricidade de Macau). The app works entirely offline. There is no account and no sign-in.

2. Data we collect

We do not collect any data. The app has no server, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter — project names, room sizes, appliances and results — is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Purchases. The optional RenoEasy Pro subscription is processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Your subscription status is read on your device from your App Store receipt only, to unlock Pro features.

3. Data retention and deletion

Your projects stay on your device until you delete them in the app or delete the app itself, which removes all of its local data. Subscription records held by Apple are governed by Apple's policies.

4. Children

RenoEasy is a general household planning tool. It is not directed at children.

5. Reference only

All figures in the app are estimates for planning reference only, calculated from public data published by CEM. Actual charges, approved capacity and installation requirements are determined by CEM. Always confirm with CEM or a licensed electrical contractor before applying or carrying out works.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version will always be posted on this page. Questions: appchallengek@gmail.com

RenoEasy 私隱政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 31 日

1. 本 App 的運作

裝修易 RenoEasy 根據澳門電力股份有限公司（澳電）公開的收費及指引資料，協助你估算家居裝修所需的訂定功率、接駁費用及冷氣機大小。本 App 完全離線運作，無需帳戶、無需登入。

2. 我們收集的資料

我們不收集任何資料。本 App 沒有伺服器、沒有數據分析、沒有廣告。你輸入的一切內容（項目名稱、房間面積、電器及計算結果）只儲存在你的裝置上、本 App 自己的儲存空間內，不會傳送給我們或任何第三方。

購買。可選購的 RenoEasy Pro 訂閱由 Apple 經 App Store 處理，我們不會接收或儲存你的付款卡資料。你的訂閱狀態只會在你的裝置上由 App Store 收據讀取，用作解鎖 Pro 功能。

3. 資料保留與刪除

你的項目會保留在你的裝置上，直至你在 App 內刪除，或刪除本 App（屆時其所有本地資料會一併移除）。由 Apple 保存的訂閱紀錄受 Apple 的政策約束。

4. 兒童

RenoEasy 是一般家居規劃工具，並非為兒童而設。

5. 只供參考

本 App 的所有數字均為根據澳電公開資料計算的估算，只供規劃參考。實際收費、獲批容量及安裝要求由澳電決定。申請或施工前，請先向澳電或持牌電業承辦商確認。

6. 修訂與聯絡

我們可能不時修訂本政策，現行版本會一直刊登於本頁。如有查詢：appchallengek@gmail.com
