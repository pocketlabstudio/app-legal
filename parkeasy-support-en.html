ParkEasy Macau Support

Last Updated: 26 November 2026 · Developer: PocketLab

ParkEasy Macau provides real-time public carpark space availability across Macau with map and list views, full-screen Cruise Mode, trends and forecasts, parked-car notes, home-screen Widgets, Lock Screen Live Activities, and CarPlay support.

FAQ

Where does parking data come from and how often does it update?

All Macau public carpark availability and static facility data is sourced from the Macao Transport Bureau (DSAT) open data via data.gov.mo. While the app is open, live space counts refresh automatically every 15 seconds, and you can also refresh manually via the map button or pull-to-refresh in the list and detail views. Carparks marked as paused indicate that official real-time reporting is temporarily suspended; always follow on-site signage.

What is the difference between Free and ParkEasy Pro?

Free includes: real-time map and list views for all Macau public carparks, filters and Cantonese voice search, tariff/height-limit details and today's trend chart, Cruise Mode, on-device parked-car records with photos, up to 5 favorite carparks, 1 home-screen widget group, and CarPlay support.

ParkEasy Pro (HK$18/month or HK$128/year, with a 7-day free trial for eligible new subscribers) unlocks: low-space push alerts for favorite carparks, +1h/+2h parking availability forecasts and suggested parking windows, unlimited favorites, unlimited widget groups, up to 3 simultaneous Lock Screen Live Activity pins, and an ad-free experience.

A separate one-time purchase, "Lifetime Ad-Free" (HK$228), permanently removes in-app ads without including Pro subscription features.

How do I restore purchases or cancel a subscription?

Restore: Open Profile › Upgrade to Pro (or Plan Details), scroll to the bottom, and tap "Restore Purchases".

Manage or cancel: Open iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions › ParkEasy Macau. Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the free trial or current billing period.

How do I set up Low-Space Alerts?

With ParkEasy Pro active, tap the heart icon on any carparks you want to watch, then go to Profile › Low-Space Alerts, toggle "Enable Low-Space Alerts" on, and allow iOS notifications. You can customize the remaining car-space threshold and quiet hours (default 23:00–07:00). Switching the toggle off stops alerts and deletes your push registration from our server.

Can I use the app without granting location access?

Yes. Without location permission, the map and full list of Macau public carparks still work normally (sorted by available spaces); only distance readouts are hidden.

Are my parked-car records and photos uploaded?

No. Parked-car locations, floor/bay notes, and photos are stored strictly on your device and are never uploaded.

Important
