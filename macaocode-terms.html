用量與提問限制

訂閱用量

  • 免費版：每月 5 條成功作答的提問。
  • Pro 月費計劃：每月 150 條成功作答的提問。
  • Pro 年費計劃：每月 300 條成功作答的提問。
  • 只有成功作答的提問才計算用量。未能作答的提問（包括所問規範未有收錄、本程式未能找到可直接作答的條文）不計算用量。

提問限制

為防止自動化程式濫用本服務，提問設有以下限制：

  • 每個安裝每小時最多提出 15 條問題。
  • 如一小時內未能作答的提問達到 10 條，或一日內達到 20 條（免費版為 10 條），系統會暫停接受提問，直至該時段結束為止。
  • 如一日內未能作答的提問連續超過 10 日達到上限，系統會視為異常使用，暫停該安裝的提問服務。被暫停的用戶可電郵至 appchallengek@gmail.com 查詢及申訴，經核實後可恢復服務。
  • 為保障服務穩定，本公司可因應系統負荷暫時限制提問量。

上述限制只影響提問服務；條文庫及裝置內已儲存的內容不受影響。

退款

訂閱由 Apple 處理付款。如需退款，請按 Apple 的政策及程序向 Apple 申請。

Usage and Question Limits

Subscription Usage

  • Free plan: 5 successfully answered questions per month.
  • Pro Monthly plan: 150 successfully answered questions per month.
  • Pro Annual plan: 300 successfully answered questions per month.
  • Only successfully answered questions count towards usage. Questions that cannot be answered (including where the code asked about is not included, or where this app cannot find a provision that directly answers the question) do not count towards usage.

Question Limits

To prevent abuse of this service by automated programs, the following limits apply to questions:

  • Each installation may ask a maximum of 15 questions per hour.
  • If unanswered questions reach 10 within one hour, or 20 within one day (10 for the free plan), the system will stop accepting questions until the end of that period.
  • If unanswered questions reach the daily limit for more than 10 consecutive days, the system will treat this as abnormal use and suspend the question service for that installation. Suspended users may email appchallengek@gmail.com with enquiries and appeals; the service may be restored after verification.
  • To maintain service stability, we may temporarily limit the volume of questions in response to system load.

The above limits affect the question service only; the provisions library and content already saved on the device are not affected.

Refunds

Payment for subscriptions is processed by Apple. To request a refund, please apply to Apple in accordance with Apple's policies and procedures.
