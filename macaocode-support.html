Common questions

Restore a purchase: Open Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchase". Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID transaction and can be restored on any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

Free questions: The free plan includes 5 answered questions per month. Only questions the app actually answers count toward the quota — if a regulation is not in the database and the app says so, that does not count. The quota resets each month.

Manage or cancel a subscription: Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open iOS Settings › your Apple ID › Subscriptions to change or cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Offline use: The built-in statute library can be searched without a network connection. Asking a question requires an internet connection, because answers are generated on our server.

Scope of answers: MACAO CODE covers Macau regulations only. Every answer cites the official gazette text; if a regulation is not in the database, the app will tell you instead of guessing. Answers are for reference only and are not legal advice — always verify against the official gazette text for statutory submissions.

Favourites and history not syncing: Sync uses your personal iCloud (CloudKit). Make sure you are signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive enabled for MACAO CODE, then reopen the app and give it a moment to download your records.

常見問題

恢復購買：在 App 內開啟「設定」並點按「恢復購買」。訂閱跟你的 Apple ID 交易綁定，喺以同一 Apple ID 登入的任何裝置都可恢復。

免費問題：免費版每月包括 5 條已作答問題。只有 App 真正作答的問題才計入額度——若某法規未收錄於資料庫而 App 如實告知，該次不計。額度每月重置。

管理或取消訂閱：訂閱由 Apple 處理。開啟 iOS「設定 › 你的 Apple ID › 訂閱」即可更改或取消。刪除 App 不會取消訂閱。

離線使用：內置法規庫可離線搜尋。發問需要網絡連線，因為答案由我們的伺服器生成。

答案範圍：MACAO CODE 只涵蓋澳門法規。每個答案均附官方公報原文出處；法規未收錄時，App 會如實告知而不會猜測。答案僅供參考，並不構成法律意見——用於法定提交前請對照官方公報原文核實。

收藏及歷史未同步：同步使用你的個人 iCloud（CloudKit）。請確認已登入 iCloud 並為 MACAO CODE 啟用 iCloud Drive，再重新開啟 App 並稍候片刻下載記錄。
