ParkEasy Macau Privacy Policy

Effective Date / Last Updated: 26 November 2026 · Developer: PocketLab

In short: no account, no tracking, minimal data

ParkEasy Macau (developed by PocketLab) requires no account or sign-in, and includes no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party tracking. Your favorite carparks, parked-car records, and preferences stay on your device. Only if you explicitly enable Pro "Low-Space Alerts" does the app send your anonymous device push token, watched carparks, and threshold to our server—solely to deliver those alerts, and deleted as soon as you turn the feature off.

If you use voice search, audio is processed by Apple's Speech Recognition service under iOS; we do not record or store your voice. The app never reads your contacts, health data, or clipboard.

Parking data source

Real-time public carpark availability and facility information in Macau is sourced from the Macao Transport Bureau (DSAT) open data via data.gov.mo, cached and served through PocketLab's backend. Fetching carpark and trend data transmits no personally identifiable information.

Low-space push alerts (Pro)

Low-Space Alerts is an optional ParkEasy Pro feature and is off by default. When you enable it in the app (Profile › Low-Space Alerts) and grant notification permission, your device push token, watched carpark IDs, threshold, and quiet-hours preference are stored on our server solely to send push notifications when spaces drop below your threshold. They are never used for advertising, marketing, or profiling. Turning off the alert toggle instructs our server to delete your push token and alert settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

ParkEasy Pro monthly/annual subscriptions and the one-time "Lifetime Ad-Free" purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. We only receive entitlement status from Apple to unlock features on your device; we never see your name, Apple ID, or payment details.

Deleting your data

  • On-device data: clear your parked-car record, remove favorites or widget groups inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all local data and photos.
  • Server push alert data: switch off "Enable Low-Space Alerts" in Profile › Low-Space Alerts to delete your device push token and watchlist from our server.
  • System permissions: revoke Location, Photos, Microphone, Speech Recognition, or Notification permissions anytime in iPhone Settings › ParkEasy Macau.

Children

ParkEasy Macau is a public parking availability app for drivers. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Important

Real-time space counts, trends, forecasts, and tariff details are provided for reference only. Please follow on-site signage and conditions, and never operate your phone while driving.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and note material updates in the app's release notes.
