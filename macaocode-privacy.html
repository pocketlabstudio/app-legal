MACAO CODE Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

1. What the app does

MACAO CODE answers questions about Macau electromechanical regulations, with every answer cited to the official gazette text. To do this, the app sends your question to our server. There is no account and no sign-in.

2. Data we process

Your questions. When you ask a question, the question text is sent to our server and processed by our third-party AI service provider to generate the answer. Your questions and their answers are kept on our server for 7 days; you can delete them at any time in the app under More › Privacy › Delete answers stored on the server. Questions that are answered, together with their answers, are also kept in a server cache for 30 days so that repeat questions can be answered faster; the cache is keyed by a one-way hash of the question. Anonymous processing records are kept for 90 days and do not contain your install identifier.

Device Keychain. The app stores its install identifier, the credential used to connect securely to our server, and its App Attest key identifier in your device's iOS Keychain, so that it can identify this install and keep its connection to our server secure. These items stay on this device only and are not synced through iCloud or transferred to another device.

Install and entitlement identifiers. The app uses Apple App Attest to verify genuine installs, and a hashed (HMAC) form of your App Store transaction ID to apply the free monthly quota and your subscription entitlement. These identifiers cannot be used by us to learn your name, email address or Apple ID.

Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. Apple notifies our server of your subscription status (for example, active, renewed or expired) so the app can apply the right entitlement.

Your in-app content. Favourites and history are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your personal iCloud account via Apple CloudKit, under your own Apple ID. We cannot access this data.

What we do not collect. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no third-party tracking.

3. How data is used

Data is used only to answer your questions, to apply the free quota and subscription entitlements, and to keep the service secure and within its operating limits (for example, rate limiting and cost controls). We do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties for marketing.

4. Data retention and deletion

Your questions and answers are deleted from our server automatically after 7 days, and you can delete them immediately in the app under More › Privacy › Delete answers stored on the server. The answer cache (30 days) and anonymous processing records (90 days) are not linked to your identity, so they cannot be deleted for an individual user; they are deleted automatically when their retention period ends. Deleting the app removes its local data, but iOS does not clear Keychain items when an app is deleted: the install identifier and connection credential remain on the device, and the app registers again if you reinstall it. Data stored in your iCloud can be managed or deleted through your Apple ID settings. Subscription records held by Apple are governed by Apple's policies.

5. Children

MACAO CODE is a professional reference tool for engineers and practitioners. It is not directed at children.

6. Reference only

Answers are provided for reference only and are not legal advice. Always verify against the official gazette text before relying on an answer for statutory submissions or works.

7. Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version will always be posted on this page. Questions: appchallengek@gmail.com

MACAO CODE 私隱政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 2 日

1. 本 App 的運作

MACAO CODE 解答有關澳門機電法規的問題，每個答案均附官方公報原文出處。為此，App 會將你的問題傳送至我們的伺服器處理。本 App 無需帳戶、無需登入。

2. 我們處理的資料

你的提問。你發問時，問題文字會傳送至我們的伺服器，並由我們的第三方人工智能服務供應商處理以生成答案。你的提問及答案會在伺服器保留 7 日，你可隨時在 App「更多 › 私隱 › 刪除伺服器上已存答案」即時刪除。已作答的問題及其答案亦會在伺服器快取保留 30 日，以便重複問題可更快作答；快取以問題的單向雜湊值為鍵，我們無法從快取鍵還原你的身份。匿名處理紀錄保留 90 日，不含安裝識別碼。

裝置鑰匙圈（Keychain）。App 會在你裝置的 iOS 鑰匙圈內儲存安裝識別碼、與我們伺服器安全連線所用的憑證，以及 App Attest 金鑰編號，用於識別此安裝及維持與伺服器的安全連線。這些項目只保存在此裝置，不會經 iCloud 同步，亦不會轉移至其他裝置。

安裝及權益識別碼。App 使用 Apple App Attest 驗證安裝真偽，並以你 App Store 交易編號的雜湊（HMAC）形式識別碼，執行免費每月額度及訂閱權益。我們無法透過這些識別碼得知你的姓名、電郵或 Apple ID。

購買。訂閱由 Apple 經 App Store 處理，我們不會收到或儲存你的付款卡資料。Apple 會通知我們的伺服器你的訂閱狀態（例如生效、續期或到期），以便 App 套用正確權益。

你的 App 內內容。收藏及歷史記錄儲存在你的裝置，以及（如你使用 iCloud）你以自己 Apple ID 登入的個人 iCloud 帳戶（經 Apple CloudKit）。我們無法存取這些資料。

我們不收集的資料。我們不收集你的位置、通訊錄、相片或廣告識別碼。本 App 沒有廣告、沒有第三方分析、沒有第三方追蹤。

3. 資料用途

資料只用於回答你的問題、執行免費額度及訂閱權益，以及維持服務安全及運作上限（例如速率限制及成本控制）。我們不會出售、出租或與第三方分享你的資料作市場推廣用途。

4. 保留及刪除

你的提問及答案會在伺服器保留 7 日後自動刪除，你亦可在 App「更多 › 私隱 › 刪除伺服器上已存答案」即時刪除。答案快取（30 日）及匿名處理紀錄（90 日）不與你的身份關聯，無法按個別用戶刪除，會在保留期屆滿後自動刪除。刪除 App 即移除其本地資料，惟 iOS 刪除 App 時不會清除鑰匙圈項目：安裝識別碼及連線憑證會留在裝置上；重新安裝後，App 會重新登記。iCloud 內的資料可經你的 Apple ID 設定管理或刪除。Apple 持有的訂閱記錄受 Apple 的政策規管。

5. 兒童

MACAO CODE 是供工程師及從業員使用的專業查證工具，並非為兒童而設。

6. 僅供參考

答案僅供參考，並不構成法律意見。用於法定提交或工程前，請務必對照官方公報原文核實。

7. 修訂及聯絡

我們可能不時更新本政策，最新版本會一直刊登於本頁。查詢：appchallengek@gmail.com
