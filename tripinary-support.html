Common questions

Restore a purchase: Open the paywall or Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". Your subscription is tied to your Apple ID and can be restored on any device signed in with the same Apple ID.

Manage or cancel a subscription: Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, go to Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or use "Manage Subscription" in the app's Settings. Deleting your Tripinary account does not cancel your subscription.

Sync: Trips are stored on your device and sync to your account when you are online. Editing offline works fully — changes upload automatically when the connection returns. If a trip was changed on another device at the same time, the app keeps the server version and parks your edit in "Unapplied changes" for 7 days so you can re-apply it.

AI points: Subscribers receive a monthly AI points allowance that resets each billing month; each AI task costs the points shown before you confirm. Free accounts can watch one rewarded ad per day to earn one AI chat round.

Delete your account: In the app: Settings › Delete Account. This removes your account and trip data from our server. It does not cancel your Apple subscription (see above).

常見問題

還原購買：在 App 內的付費頁或「設定」點按「還原購買」。你的訂閱與 Apple ID 綁定，可在登入同一 Apple ID 的任何裝置還原。

管理或取消訂閱：訂閱由 Apple 管理。在 iPhone 前往「設定 › 你的姓名 › 訂閱」，或使用 App 設定內的「管理訂閱」。刪除 Tripinary 帳戶並不會取消訂閱。

同步：行程儲存在你的裝置，連線時會同步至你的帳戶。離線編輯可正常使用，恢復連線後會自動上傳。如行程同時在另一裝置被更改，App 會採用伺服器版本，並將你的改動保留在「未套用修改」7 日，讓你可重新套用。

AI 點數：訂閱用戶每月（訂閱週期）會獲得 AI 點數額度；每個 AI 任務的點數成本會在你確認前顯示。免費帳戶每日可觀看一次獎勵廣告，換取一輪 AI 對話。

刪除帳戶：在 App 內前往「設定 › 刪除帳戶」，會將你的帳戶及行程資料從我們的伺服器移除。此操作不會取消你的 Apple 訂閱（見上）。
