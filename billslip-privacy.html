BillSlip Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

1. Data We Collect

BillSlip （開單易） does not operate any servers, has no user accounts, and does not collect your data. Everything you enter in the app — your business details, clients, quotations, invoices, receipts and payment records — is stored only on your device. We, as the developer, cannot access this data.

2. Sharing PDFs

When you export or share a PDF document, the file is handed to the app or person you choose through the iOS share sheet (for example WhatsApp or email). That sharing is initiated entirely by you; nothing is sent to us.

3. Notifications

Payment-due reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No remote push notifications are used, and no notification data leaves your device.

4. Subscriptions

BillSlip Pro is an auto-renewable subscription processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your App Store account settings.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete any document or record in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its local data from your device. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on request.

6. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version will always be posted on this page. Questions: appchallengek@gmail.com

開單易私隱政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 27 日

1. 我們收集的資料

開單易（BillSlip）沒有任何伺服器、沒有用戶帳戶、不收集你的資料。你在 app 內輸入的一切 — 商號資料、客戶、報價單、請款單、收據與收款記錄 — 只儲存在你的裝置上。我們作為開發者，無法存取這些資料。

2. 分享 PDF

當你匯出或分享 PDF 文件時，檔案會經 iOS 分享表交給你選擇的 app 或聯絡人（例如 WhatsApp 或電郵）。分享完全由你主動發起，沒有任何內容會傳送給我們。

3. 通知

請款到期提醒是排定在你裝置上的本地通知。本 app 不使用遙距推送通知，通知資料不會離開你的裝置。

4. 訂閱

開單易 Pro 是經 Apple App Store 處理的自動續訂訂閱。我們不會接收或儲存你的付款資料。你可以隨時在 App Store 帳戶設定中管理或取消訂閱。

5. 資料保留與刪除

你可以隨時在 app 內刪除任何文件或記錄。刪除 app 會移除其在本機的一切資料。由於我們不持有你的資料副本，沒有需要我們代為刪除的內容。

6. 修訂與聯絡

本政策可能不時更新，最新版本會一直刊登於本頁。查詢：appchallengek@gmail.com
